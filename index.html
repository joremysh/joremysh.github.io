About

I'm a senior software engineer focused on the backend of software that runs factories — manufacturing execution, quality management and the services around them. Most of my work is in Golang on highly available, microservice-based systems.

My path has been unusual: I started as a mechanical engineer designing automotive displays, moved into factory equipment and robotics software, then into semiconductor test tooling, and eventually into large-scale distributed systems for Tesla and TSMC.

Along the way I've led small teams, mentored new engineers, driven agile transformations, and contributed to Kubernetes. I'm now based in Berlin, working on factory software at Gigafactory Berlin-Brandenburg.
